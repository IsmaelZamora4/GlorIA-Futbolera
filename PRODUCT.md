# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Football followers who want context around matches and competitions. This audience is inferred from the product description and the user-approved `/como-funciona` brief.

## Product Purpose

GlorIA Futbolera is a Spanish-first portfolio web project for exploring football matches, competitions, and pre-match statistical analysis.

## Positioning

It presents football data and statistical estimates with their context and limitations. It does not promise results or operate as a betting service.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The landing page, contact page, legal information, and `/como-funciona` are public.
- Access to the football analysis product requires an authenticated account, as confirmed by the user.
- GlorIA does not accept bets, process payments, hold funds, or execute wagers.
- Sample values and previews must be identified as illustrative when they are not live product data.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name GlorIA Futbolera and Spanish as the primary language.
- Preserve the existing dark football-stadium and violet visual identity.
- The `/como-funciona` hero reuses the homepage wallpaper and downward fade; its footer reuses the existing footer and upward fade.
- The attached `/como-funciona` mockup is the visual reference for this page.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product requirements and statistical analysis: `docs/GlorIA_Futbolera_MVP.md` and the methodology implementation.
- Dataset metadata: `data/datasets.ts`.
- The homepage match preview is an illustrative sample: `data/landing-match-preview.json`.
- No testimonials, user research results, or verified performance claims are established by the available project evidence.

## Product Principles

- Explain match context clearly.
- Distinguish observed data from statistical estimates and illustrative samples.
- Describe uncertainty and data limitations honestly.
- Do not present estimates as guarantees or betting advice.
